The core resolves the following ready-made libraries through Gradle/Maven.
The Fabric distribution embeds its core and mathematical runtime as nested JARs;
the standalone editor bundles the required classes. Third-party licenses and
notices are retained in META-INF/licenses and in the original nested libraries.
